Aniya Essentials Product Photography Folder
-------------------------------------------

To use your actual product photographs on the website, simply place your photo files here with these names:

1. Hair Oil photo:
   public/images/hair-oil.jpg
   (or .png / .webp)

2. Hair Spray photo:
   public/images/hair-spray.jpg
   (or .png / .webp)

When present, the website automatically displays your actual photos with smooth drop shadows.
If no photo is placed, the polished vector apothecary bottle placeholder is displayed seamlessly.
